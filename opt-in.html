Opt-In Method and Consent Records

Program: "Jarvis Voice," operated by Adam D Goldberg (sole proprietor, Plainview, New York) on the phone number +1 (516) 363-0805.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What this program is — and is not

Jarvis Voice is a personal, non-commercial messaging line. It is the operator's personal-assistant line, used only for communications with the operator himself and his immediate family and household members. The recipient list is a small, closed list (the operator plus his wife and daughters). It is not a business or marketing program: it sends no advertising, no promotions, and no messages to the public, and it never uses purchased, rented, or third-party lists. There is no public sign-up form and no way for a member of the public to join.

Every recipient consents (opts in) before the program sends them any SMS message, using one of the two methods below.

Call to action — the exact opt-in language

The following call-to-action language is what every recipient hears or reads before opting in, whether they opt in by texting first (method 1) or verbally (method 2). It contains the program description, the identity of the individual represented, the fees disclosure in the required wording, the message frequency, the policy links, and the opt-out and help terms:

"By texting START to +1 (516) 363-0805, or by verbally agreeing using the script below, you consent to receive SMS from Adam D Goldberg (Jarvis Voice) for personal-assistant and household communications — reminders, delivery and schedule updates, and replies to things you ask the assistant. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt-out or HELP for assistance. Our Privacy Policy can be found here https://adamdgoldberg-maker.github.io/jarvis-voice/privacy-policy.html and our Terms of Service can be found here https://adamdgoldberg-maker.github.io/jarvis-voice/terms-and-conditions.html"

Checkbox and phone-field requirements: this program has no website sign-up form, no checkbox, and no phone-number field of any kind — so there is no pre-checked box, and no phone number is ever collected through a form, mandatory or otherwise. The only opt-in mechanisms are the recipient's own first text message (method 1) and the recipient's spoken "yes" after the script below (method 2).

Opt-in method 1 — recipient-initiated first text (primary method)

  1. The operator tells a family or household member the Jarvis Voice number, +1 (516) 363-0805, in person or in an existing conversation, using the call-to-action language above: that texting the number opts them in to personal-assistant and household messages from Adam D Goldberg (Jarvis Voice), that message frequency varies, that Message and data rates may apply, that they can reply STOP at any time to end messages or HELP for assistance, and where the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service can be found.
  2. The recipient initiates contact: from their own mobile phone, the recipient sends the first text message (the word START, or any first message) to +1 (516) 363-0805. No SMS is sent by the program to that number before this step.
  3. That inbound message is the consent record. It is stored automatically in the UniFi Talk message history with the recipient's mobile number, the date and time, and the message content, and is retained for as long as the recipient remains opted in.
  4. Only after that opt-in does the program send a confirmation message and any further messages. Confirmation message: "Jarvis Voice: You're opted in to messages from Adam Goldberg's personal assistant line. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to end."

Opt-in method 2 — verbal opt-in in person, with a written record

Used when a family or household member is with the operator in person and prefers to agree verbally rather than text first.

  1. The operator and the recipient have the following exchange, word for word (this is the transcript of the verbal opt-in):

    Operator: "This is Adam Goldberg. As part of my personal assistant line, Jarvis Voice, I can send you text messages from the number 516-363-0805 with household and assistant updates — reminders, delivery and schedule updates, and replies to things you ask it. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. At any time you can get more help by replying HELP to these texts, or you can opt out completely by replying STOP. This is not marketing, and your number will not be shared for marketing. Our Privacy Policy can be found at https://adamdgoldberg-maker.github.io/jarvis-voice/privacy-policy.html and our Terms of Service can be found at https://adamdgoldberg-maker.github.io/jarvis-voice/terms-and-conditions.html. Would you like to receive these texts at your number? Please say yes or no."

    Recipient: "Yes." (A "no" ends the process and the number is not added. The recipient states the mobile number they want messages sent to.)

    Operator: "Great! I will send you a text message to confirm your enrollment here shortly."

  2. Before any SMS is sent to that number, the operator records the consent in the consent log: recipient name, mobile number, date and time, method ("verbal, in person"), and the script above. The log is kept by the operator and a sample completed record appears below.
  3. The first SMS sent after a verbal opt-in is the same confirmation message shown in method 1, step 4.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out; one confirmation is sent and no further messages follow unless the recipient opts in again. Reply HELP for assistance.
