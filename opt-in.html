Opt-In Method and Consent Records

Program: "Jarvis Voice," operated by Adam D Goldberg (sole proprietor, Plainview, New York) on the phone number +1 (516) 363-0805.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What this program is — and is not

Jarvis Voice is a personal, non-commercial messaging line. It is the operator's personal-assistant line, used only for communications with the operator himself and his immediate family and household members. The recipient list is a small, closed list (the operator plus his wife and daughters). It is not a business or marketing program: it sends no advertising, no promotions, and no messages to the public, and it never uses purchased, rented, or third-party lists. There is no public sign-up form and no way for a member of the public to join.

Every recipient consents (opts in) before the program sends them any SMS message, using one of the two methods below.

Opt-in method 1 — recipient-initiated first text (primary method)

  1. The operator tells a family or household member the Jarvis Voice number, +1 (516) 363-0805, in person or in an existing conversation, and explains that texting the number opts them in to assistant and household messages from it, that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, and that they can reply STOP at any time to end messages.
  2. The recipient initiates contact: from their own mobile phone, the recipient sends the first text message (the word START, or any first message) to +1 (516) 363-0805. No SMS is sent by the program to that number before this step.
  3. That inbound message is the consent record. It is stored automatically in the UniFi Talk message history with the recipient's mobile number, the date and time, and the message content, and is retained for as long as the recipient remains opted in.
  4. Only after that opt-in does the program send a confirmation message and any further messages. Confirmation message: "Jarvis Voice: You're opted in to messages from Adam Goldberg's personal assistant line. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to end."

Opt-in method 2 — verbal opt-in in person, with a written record

Used when a family or household member is with the operator in person and prefers to agree verbally rather than text first.

  1. The operator reads the recipient the following script, word for word:
    "I'm setting up a personal assistant text line, Jarvis Voice, on the number 516-363-0805. If you give me your mobile number and agree, that line may text you household and assistant messages — for example reminders, delivery and schedule updates, and replies to things you ask it. Message frequency varies, and your carrier's message and data rates may apply. This is not marketing and your number will not be shared for marketing. You can reply STOP at any time to stop the messages, or HELP for help. Do you agree to receive these texts at your number?"
  2. The recipient verbally agrees (or declines — a decline ends the process and the number is not added) and states their mobile number.
  3. Before any SMS is sent to that number, the operator records the consent in the consent log: recipient name, mobile number, date and time, method ("verbal, in person"), and the script above. The log is kept by the operator and a sample completed record appears below.
  4. The first SMS sent after a verbal opt-in is the same confirmation message shown in method 1, step 4.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out; one confirmation is sent and no further messages follow unless the recipient opts in again. Reply HELP for assistance.
