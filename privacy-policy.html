Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Adam D Goldberg (sole proprietor, Plainview, New York) ("we," "us") handles information in connection with the "Jarvis Voice" messaging program, operated on the phone number +1 (516) 363-0805 (the "Program").

What the Program is

The Program is a personal-assistant messaging line used for communications with the operator's household and family members, and for assistant notifications those individuals have asked to receive. It is not a marketing program. Messages are sent only to individuals who have provided their mobile number to the operator and consented to receive messages from this line.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone numbers of message recipients, provided directly by the recipients or by the operator at a recipient's request.
  • Message content exchanged between recipients and the assistant line.
  • Basic delivery and interaction records (for example, whether a message was delivered, and opt-out requests).

We do not collect account credentials, payment information, or precise location data through this Program.

How information is used

  • To send and receive the household and assistant messages described above.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the messaging line.
  • To honor opt-out and help requests.

Sharing

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers strictly as needed to operate the messaging service (for example, the telephony and messaging carriers that deliver the messages), and as required by law, regulation, or legal process. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text message services necessary to deliver messages.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out of further messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies based on your interactions with the assistant. Message and data rates may apply.

Data retention

Message records are retained only as long as needed to operate the line and are deleted on request, subject to carrier and legal retention requirements.
